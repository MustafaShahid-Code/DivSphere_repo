Questions

Before you reach out.

How is a project priced — fixed price or time and materials?

Both, depending on how well-defined the work is. A clearly scoped deliverable (a rebuild, a defined integration, a security audit) is usually fixed price, agreed after a short discovery phase. Ongoing product development or an embedded team works better as time and materials with a monthly cap. We'll recommend one during the first call and explain the trade-offs — we don't lock you into a model that doesn't fit.

How long does a typical engagement take?

A focused project (an audit, an MVP, a single integration) usually runs 6–10 weeks. Larger builds — ERP rollouts, platform rebuilds, multi-phase migrations — typically run 4–9 months, delivered in two-week cycles so you have a working build in hand from sprint one rather than waiting until the end.

We already have an in-house engineering team — can you still help?

Yes, this is a large part of what we do. We slot in as an extension of an existing team for a specific gap — a cloud migration, a security review, extra capacity during a crunch — reporting into your process rather than replacing it. See IT Outsourcing under Services.

Do you work with early-stage companies, or only larger organizations?

Both. Startups typically come to us for a first product build or to harden something built quickly pre-funding; larger organizations more often bring ERP, data, or security work. Pricing and process adjust to the size of the engagement either way.

Who owns the code and IP once the project ships?

You do. Every engagement is covered by an NDA before any details are shared, and the contract assigns IP in the deliverables to you on final payment — there's no vendor lock-in to us specifically.

Where is DivSphere based, and do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

We're headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan, and deliver for clients across South Asia, the Gulf, and beyond — remotely, with overlap hours agreed up front for standups and reviews. Enquiries get a response within one business day.
