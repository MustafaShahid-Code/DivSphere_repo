Clients come to us mid-migration more often than at the start of one — a lift-and-shift that stalled, a cost bill that doubled, an environment nobody fully documented. You'll be the person who walks in, makes sense of what's there, and designs the target state the rest of the delivery team builds toward.

## What you'll do

- Assess existing client infrastructure and produce a target cloud architecture — compute, networking, storage, identity — with a migration path that doesn't require a weekend outage
- Lead cost and security reviews on AWS, Azure, and GCP environments, and turn findings into a prioritized remediation plan a client can actually execute
- Design for the failure modes that matter to each client's business, not a generic best-practices checklist — a logistics platform's availability requirements aren't a SaaS dashboard's
- Pair with engineering leads during build to keep implementation honest to the architecture, and adjust the architecture when reality disagrees with the diagram
- Present architecture decisions directly to client technical leadership and defend the tradeoffs

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years in cloud architecture or senior infrastructure engineering, with production experience on at least two of AWS, Azure, GCP
- A track record of migrations you can actually describe in detail — what broke, what the rollback plan was, what you'd do differently
- Solid grounding in networking, IAM, and cost modeling — the parts of cloud architecture that don't show up in a certification exam
- Infrastructure-as-code experience (Terraform, CloudFormation, or Pulumi) — we don't hand-click through consoles for anything that ships
- Comfort being the most senior technical voice in a client meeting

## What you get

Direct exposure to a range of industries and infrastructure maturity levels, architectural ownership from day one, and a team that treats "it works" and "it's designed to keep working" as two different bars.
