## The challenge

The existing loyalty app had a 2.1 star rating and most reviews described the same problem: redeeming points took six screens and frequently failed at the last step. Customers were signing up and never returning.

## Our approach

We watched twelve customers try to redeem points on the old app before designing anything. Every one of them hesitated at the same two screens. The rebuild collapsed redemption to two steps and made point balance visible from the home screen instead of three levels deep.

## The outcome

Weekly active users more than tripled within six months of launch. The store rating rose from 2.1 to 4.6, and repeat purchase rate among app users increased 58%.
