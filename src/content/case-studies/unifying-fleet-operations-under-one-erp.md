## The challenge

Six regional offices each ran dispatch on their own spreadsheets and local conventions. Headquarters had no consolidated view, month-end reporting took eleven days, and no two offices calculated utilisation the same way.

## Our approach

We started with the process, not the software — three weeks on site across four countries mapping how dispatch actually worked versus how the manual said it worked. The rollout went country by country, each one fully live and stable before the next began.

## The outcome

All four countries now run one system with a shared definition of every metric. Month-end reporting takes under a day, and dispatch cycle time improved by nearly a third.
