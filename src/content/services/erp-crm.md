ERP and CRM rollouts rarely stall because the software doesn't work. They stall because change management, data migration trust, and honest scoping got less attention than the technical configuration — and by the time that's visible, the project is already off track.

## What's included

Requirements gathering that maps your actual workflows before configuration starts, rather than forcing your process to fit the software's defaults and calling the mismatch a "training issue" later. Data migration validated against what your own team knows to be operationally true, not just checked for technical integrity — because the first wrong number a long-tenured employee spots is often what undoes trust in the whole system. Role-based training that covers real judgment calls and edge cases, continuing past go-live rather than stopping the week before it. Integration with the other systems ERP and CRM need to talk to, so data doesn't end up re-entered by hand between them. And a change-management plan with a named owner and visible executive sponsorship that continues after launch, not just during kickoff.

## When to bring us in

The earlier we're involved — ideally before a vendor or platform is locked in — the more the implementation plan can be built around your actual workflows instead of retrofitted to them afterward.
