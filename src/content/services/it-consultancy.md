Not every technology decision needs a project team — sometimes what's actually needed is an experienced, independent perspective in the room before a decision gets made, not a vendor brought in to execute one that's already locked.

## What's included

Ongoing advisory access for IT leadership, structured around your actual decision calendar rather than a fixed generic engagement. Budget planning grounded in realistic cost modeling — including the costs that are easy to underestimate, like ongoing maintenance and the true cost of technical debt left unaddressed. Staffing strategy that helps decide, deliberately, what should be built in-house versus engaged externally, rather than letting that split happen reactively project by project. Technical strategy input on architecture direction, platform choices, and where the organization's technology is likely to become a constraint before it becomes an emergency. And a genuinely independent perspective — since we're not the ones selling you the platform or the headcount, the advice isn't shaped by what we'd profit from recommending.

## When to bring us in

Whenever a significant technology or staffing decision is being weighed — the value is highest before the decision is made, not after, when an outside perspective can still change the outcome.
