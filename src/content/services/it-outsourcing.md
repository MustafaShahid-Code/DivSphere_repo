Outsourced engineering works when it genuinely operates as an extension of your team — same standards, same visibility into progress, same accountability — and fails when it operates as a black box you check in on once a sprint. The difference is almost entirely about how the engagement is structured, not about outsourcing as a model.

## What's included

Dedicated or augmented team structures, matched to whether you need a self-contained unit owning a whole workstream or individual engineers embedded directly into your existing team's process. Full visibility into progress — the same sprint boards, standups, and code review process your in-house team would use, not a black-box monthly status report. Knowledge transfer built in throughout the engagement, not reserved for a rushed handover at the end, so you're never dependent on us for changes you should be able to make yourselves. Flexible billing structured around your actual needs — dedicated capacity, project-based, or hourly augmentation. And a genuine off-ramp: documentation and a codebase your own team can pick up and run with if the engagement ends.

## When to bring us in

Outsourcing fits best for genuinely bounded work or for scaling capacity quickly without the multi-month timeline of hiring — talk to us early in the planning stage so the engagement model matches what you actually need, rather than defaulting to whatever's fastest to start.
