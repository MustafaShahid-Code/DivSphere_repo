A SaaS product is really two things bolted together: the software your customers use, and the operational machinery — billing, tenant isolation, onboarding, uptime — that makes running it as a service actually sustainable. Most SaaS projects that struggle have the first part right and never properly built the second.

## What's included

Multi-tenant architecture designed for real isolation between customers' data from day one, since retrofitting proper tenant separation onto a system built for a single customer is a far larger project than building it in from the start. Subscription billing integrated with a real payment processor, handling upgrades, downgrades, failed payments, and dunning without manual intervention. Self-serve onboarding that gets a new customer to value without a sales call for every signup. Usage metering and plan enforcement, so feature gates and limits actually hold. And the operational side — monitoring, alerting, and an incident response process — because SaaS customers expect uptime, not a best-effort promise.

## When to bring us in

Whether you're building a new SaaS product from scratch or converting an existing single-tenant application into a multi-tenant service, involve us before the data model is finalized — tenant isolation is one of the more expensive things to retrofit after the fact.
