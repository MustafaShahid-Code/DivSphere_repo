Off-the-shelf software is built for a generic version of your problem. Custom development exists for the parts of your business that don't fit that generic shape — a workflow specific to how your team actually operates, a customer experience that needs to be genuinely differentiated, an internal tool that replaces three spreadsheets and a shared inbox with something built for the job.

## What's included

Discovery and technical scoping before a line of code is written, so the estimate reflects the real problem rather than a guess. Architecture decisions made for where the system needs to be in two years, not just for launch day. Development in the languages and frameworks your existing team already runs, so what we hand over is something you can maintain without a rewrite. Automated testing built in from the start rather than bolted on at the end. And a handover that actually transfers knowledge — documentation, a working local setup, and time with your team, not just a repository link.

## When to bring us in

If the requirement is specific enough that no off-the-shelf product fits it cleanly, or if an internal process has outgrown the spreadsheet-and-email version of itself, that's usually the signal it's time for custom software rather than another workaround.
