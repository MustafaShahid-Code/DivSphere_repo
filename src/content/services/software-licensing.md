Software that was built for internal use often has real value to other companies — but turning it into something that can be licensed or white-labeled safely requires more than a business decision. It requires the codebase itself to support it.

## What's included

A technical audit of what it actually takes to license or white-label the software safely — separating tenant data cleanly, removing hardcoded assumptions specific to your original use case, and making branding and configuration genuinely swappable rather than requiring a code change per customer. License enforcement and activation systems appropriate to the distribution model, whether that's a license key, a usage-metered API, or a fully white-labeled deployment. Documentation written for a licensee who wasn't part of the original build, not just for your own team. And ongoing version management so licensees can be updated without each one becoming a bespoke, one-off deployment to maintain.

## When to bring us in

If you're evaluating whether internally-built software has standalone commercial value, an early technical audit is the cheapest way to find out what licensing would actually require before making promises to a potential partner or buyer.
