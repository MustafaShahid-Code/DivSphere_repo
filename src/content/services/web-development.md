A website is judged in the first two seconds — load speed, whether it works on the phone in someone's hand, whether the thing they came to do is obvious. Most underperforming sites aren't failing on design; they're failing on the fundamentals that never show up in a mockup.

## What's included

Performance built in from the architecture stage, not patched on afterward — the difference between a site that loads instantly and one that technically works but quietly loses visitors before it finishes rendering. Full search engine optimization: proper semantic markup, structured data, and content that's actually crawlable, not hidden behind client-side rendering search engines can't see. Responsive design tested on real devices, not just a browser's device emulator. Content management that lets your team publish changes without filing a ticket. And ongoing maintenance after launch — security patches, uptime monitoring, and small iterative improvements — because a website that never gets updated after go-live starts losing ground within months.

## When to bring us in

Whether it's a marketing site that needs to actually convert, a customer-facing portal, or a web application that's outgrown its current stack, the earlier performance and structure get considered — ideally before design is finalized — the less gets rebuilt later.
